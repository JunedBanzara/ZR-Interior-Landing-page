PRINCIPAL DESIGNER PROFILE

Design, Informed by Experience.

Mr. Zuber Rafik Banzara leads ZR Interior Design with a philosophy centered on architectural restraint, tactile authenticity, and rigorous turnkey execution.

OUR METHODOLOGY & WORKFLOW

From Design Intent to Built Reality.

A disciplined five-stage methodology that bridges creative concepts and on-site reality.

01

Understanding the Space

We examine the property's natural orientation, structural framework, client lifestyle rhythms, functional priorities, and acoustic characteristics before developing concepts.

02

Design Development

Translating functional requirements into circulation layouts, furniture plans, technical electrical/lighting schemes, and photorealistic 3D perspectives for comprehensive clarity.

03

Material and Detail

Curating tactile material palettes—natural veneers, Italian travertine, smoked brass hardware, textured plasters, and acoustic fabrics engineered for aesthetic longevity.

04

Execution

Direct site supervision led by our dedicated in-house craftsmen. Civil modifications, custom woodwork, MEP coordination, and acoustic finishes delivered under unified accountability.

05

Handover

Meticulous final detailing, illumination tuning, deep snag resolution, and turnkey handover of a completed living space ready for immediate residence.
